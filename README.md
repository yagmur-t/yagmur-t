# Yağmur Taşdemiroğlu

I’m a biomedical researcher with a background in cardiovascular science. My
graduate work focused on how a cardioprotective peptide breaks down in plasma
and also included *ex vivo* cardiac physiology. These days, I’m working with a
different kind of cardiovascular data as I learn to analyze RNA-seq and build
workflows using public heart failure datasets.
Outside of research, I get humbled by Elden Ring bosses, hang out with my cat, or practice Korean.\
**Languages:** Turkish (native), English (near-native) 

## Research

**[αCT11 peptide degradation study](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsptsci.4c00120)** · 2024  
I optimized LC-MS/MS sample preparation for more than 400 treated plasma samples,
analyzed peptide degradation data, and drafted the manuscript.

**[Therapeutic peptide review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejphar.2022.175192)** · 2022  
I reviewed how therapeutic peptides break down *in vivo*, how their stability
can be improved, and where they’re used clinically.

**[Virginia Tech master’s thesis](https://vtechworks.lib.vt.edu/bitstream/handle/10919/103639/Tasdemiroglu_Y_T_2021.pdf)** · 2021  
*In vitro* pharmacokinetics of αCT11 in rat plasma.

## Independent bioinformatics projects

**[Cardiac RNA-seq reanalysis](https://github.com/yagmur-t/GSE116250-DCM-RNAseq-Reanalysis)** · Completed  
I used DESeq2 to compare gene expression in 12 left ventricular samples from
non-failing and dilated cardiomyopathy hearts. The Quarto report walks through
the analysis and compares the results with the original study.

**[Full-cohort RNA-seq workflow](https://github.com/yagmur-t/GSE116250-FULL-RNAseq-Reanalysis-Pipeline)** · In progress  
I’m building a Snakemake workflow to work through all 64 samples, from raw reads
to analysis and figures. I chose the same dataset because I wanted to learn how
to build the workflow myself and see what a different analysis approach might
add to the published findings.
